# Homepage Copy

## Summary

This is draft homepage copy for the American Property Solutions pilot concept. It is written to show a clearer before-and-after direction for the business owner conversation, not as final approved public copy.

The copy uses the phase-one working direction: year-round property maintenance, clearer service groups, commercial/property-manager appeal, stronger calls to action, and cleaner local SEO language. Final public copy requires human/client approval before launch.

## Observations

- The homepage should lead with clarity, not a long service list.
- The strongest copy direction is commercial property maintenance first, with residential property needs included carefully until the client validates audience priority.
- The homepage should make the phone number and quote request path obvious.
- Trust signals should be represented as placeholders or omitted until approved.
- The copy should avoid repeated "near me" phrases and use natural local language instead.

## Assumptions

- Commercial property owners, property managers, local businesses, facilities/operations managers, and residential homeowners are potential audiences to validate.
- Property & Building Maintenance is the evergreen flagship service for the Phase Three demo. Snow plowing remains an important seasonal service, not the main flagship page.
- The Lehigh Valley/Walnutport area is the main local SEO anchor for the pilot concept.
- The New York location should be listed on contact sections but not overemphasized until confirmed.
- Testimonials, trust badges, certifications, awards, warranties, guarantees, and years-of-experience claims are not approved for use in final copy yet.

### Why the New Positioning Works

Section heading:

A Clearer Way to Present Property Services

Copy:

The current site lists many useful services, but the message is hard to follow. A stronger homepage should organize those services around the real customer need: keeping a property safe, usable, maintained, and ready for the season.

The new direction makes it easier for a visitor to answer three questions quickly:

- Does APS handle the kind of property issue I have?
- Does APS serve my area?
- How do I contact them for a quote?
