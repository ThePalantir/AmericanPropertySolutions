## Assumptions

- For the pilot concept, it is acceptable to use publicly listed facts while clearly marking claims that need approval.
- The first demo should prioritize quote requests and phone calls over careers, partner recruiting, or long company history.
- Service pages should be grouped before detailed build work begins.
- Phase-two homepage copy and SEO recommendations are draft/demo direction, not final approved public copy.
- The design spec can be used for planning, but build work still needs explicit approval.

## Recommendations

- Resolve the high-priority questions before final sitemap and final public copy.
- Use the phase-two pitch summary to ask for a short validation pass rather than a long discovery process.
- Treat trust signals as an approval checklist.
- Use the updated service priority order before choosing hero imagery, homepage hierarchy, and the first flagship service page.
- Confirm service areas before writing final metadata or considering location pages.
