# SEO Package

## Summary

This SEO package gives American Property Solutions a cleaner search foundation for the pilot demo. It replaces repeated keyword blocks with page-specific titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and image-alt guidance based on confirmed public services and the proposed sitemap.

This is not a final SEO launch package. Final service-area claims, trust claims, and page targets require client approval before public launch.

## H1 and H2 Rules

- Use one clear H1 per page.
- Do not repeat the business name as every H1 if a service-specific H1 is clearer.
- Use H2s to organize real service content, not keyword variants.
- Avoid "near me" blocks entirely.
- Correct spelling in all public text: Northampton, Nazareth, Schuylkill, line striping, within.

## Local SEO Direction

Recommended local copy approach:

- Use natural service-area language on the homepage and Contact page.
- Mention Lehigh Valley and Walnutport where relevant because they are publicly supported by existing site content.
- Include the full public addresses on the Contact page after APS confirms they are current.
- Do not create city pages until service areas and priority locations are validated.
- Do not repeat long lists of towns on every service page.
- Do not claim service coverage for eastern Pennsylvania, western New Jersey, or southern New York in final copy until APS confirms it.

## Internal Linking Plan

- Homepage should link to Services, Snow & Ice Management, Parking Lot Maintenance, Excavation & Sitework, and Contact.
- Services page should link to every service page and explain the five service buckets.
- Service pages should link to Contact and related overlapping services.
- Parking Lot Maintenance should link to Excavation & Sitework for drainage, concrete, and site prep overlap.
- Landscaping & Hardscaping should link to Excavation & Sitework for drainage, grading, and hardscape prep overlap.
- About should link to Services and Contact.
- Contact should offer service-interest selections matching the sitemap.

## Image Alt Guidance

Use descriptive, literal alt text based on the actual image shown.

Good examples:

- `APS truck clearing snow from a commercial parking lot`
- `Freshly striped parking lot at a commercial property`
- `Excavation equipment preparing a property drainage area`
- `Mulched landscape bed beside a commercial building`
- `Concrete sidewalk repair at a property entrance`

Avoid:

- Keyword-stuffed alt text such as `best snow plow contractor near me Lehigh Valley Allentown Bethlehem`
- Alt text that claims certifications, awards, guarantees, or years of experience.
- Generic alt text such as `image`, `photo`, or `service`.
- Alt text that describes something not visible in the image.

## Technical SEO Recommendations

- Use clean, stable slugs from `sitemap.md`.
- Add unique title tags and meta descriptions for every page.
- Use schema later only after business name, locations, phone, hours, and service areas are confirmed.
- Ensure forms have accessible labels.
- Keep phone CTA clickable on mobile.
- Compress service photos for fast loading.
- Remove visible repeated keyword blocks from the demo.
- Avoid thin pages for sub-services and locations.

## Recommendations

- Use the page matrix as the phase-two SEO direction for the demo.
- Validate service areas before final metadata is launched.
- Keep SEO copy useful and readable for property owners, managers, and businesses.
- Treat Snow & Ice Management as a likely high-priority page pending client confirmation.
- Defer testimonials, badges, certifications, awards, guarantees, and years-of-experience claims until approved.
