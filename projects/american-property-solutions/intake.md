- Weak or unclear messaging
- Outdated design
- Keyword stuffing
- Repeated "near me" keyword blocks

- Typos and spelling issues
- Unclear conversion path
- Repeated low-quality SEO text
- Need better commercial-property-maintenance positioning

- Need more credible, professional tone

## Recommended Strategic Repositioning Hypothesis

Instead of presenting APS as a scattered list of unrelated services, reposition it as a commercial and residential property maintenance partner with year-round capabilities.

Possible top-level positioning:

American Property Solutions helps property owners, managers, and businesses keep properties safe, functional, and well-maintained year-round through snow and ice management, parking lot maintenance, excavation/sitework, landscaping, hardscaping, and building repairs.

This is a hypothesis only and should be validated with the client.
