# Current Site Audit

> **Status update — August 7, 2026:** The earlier mixed-market direction in this file is superseded by the APS leadership-approved commercial-only direction in [commercial-only-revision-plan.md](commercial-only-revision-plan.md).

## Executive Summary

American Property Solutions has enough real service substance to support a much stronger website, but the current site weakens credibility by scattering the business across many service labels, repeating low-quality SEO text, and burying the clearest commercial property maintenance story.

The strongest improvement opportunity is to reposition the site around year-round property maintenance for commercial properties, property managers, local businesses, and qualified residential property needs. Snow and ice management appears to be a major credibility driver and should be treated as a priority service, supported by parking lot maintenance, excavation/sitework, landscaping/hardscaping, and building maintenance/repairs.

This audit is for a fast pilot concept only. Final public copy, final sitemap, service-area claims, trust badges, reviews, credentials, and years-of-experience claims require human/client approval before launch.

## Major Observations

- The home page has a useful core idea: APS is a four-season property maintenance company serving commercial property needs. That message is present but not developed into a clear offer.
- Navigation and footer links are inconsistent. The main menu includes many service pages, while the footer simplifies to a smaller set of links.
- The service list reads like a broad contractor directory instead of a focused property maintenance company.
- Commercial property maintenance appears to be the stronger positioning than a generic residential contractor pitch.
- Snow and ice management appears to have the richest existing content and strongest operational story.
- The site repeats large blocks of "near me" phrases and location/service keywords across pages.
- Several location names and service labels contain spelling or wording issues, including `Northhampton`, `Nazereth`, `Schuykill`, `Line Stripping`, and `with-in`.
- The site mixes valuable trust signals with unverified public claims. Some claims may be true, but should not be reused without approval.
- The conversion path exists but is not clean. Phone, contact form, "Learn More," "Email Us," "Get a Quote," and appointment language compete with each other.
- The contact form service dropdown does not fully match the current service menu.
- Page content often explains services in broad terms but does not make next steps, ideal customers, or service fit obvious.
- Weather widgets and repeated footer blocks add visual and content clutter without clearly improving conversion.

## Quick Wins

- Correct obvious spelling and label issues: Northampton, Nazareth, Schuylkill, line striping, within.
- Remove repeated "near me" keyword blocks from visible page content.
- Replace duplicate "Learn More" and mixed contact CTAs with one primary call and one quote CTA.
- Put phone number and quote request in the header.
- Rewrite the home hero around a clear property maintenance offer.
- Create one consolidated Services overview page with service cards that route users to the most important service pages.
- Make Snow & Ice Management the strongest service page in the pilot concept.
- Add a simple "Who we help" section for property owners, property managers, businesses, and residential homeowners if validated.
- Keep trust logos and testimonials out of final public copy until approved.

## Stronger Positioning Opportunity

Recommended pilot positioning:

American Property Solutions should be presented as a year-round property maintenance partner for commercial properties, property managers, local businesses, and qualified residential property needs in the Lehigh Valley and surrounding listed service areas.

The strongest organizing idea is not "we do everything." It is: APS helps properties stay safe, functional, and maintained through snow and ice management, parking lot maintenance, sitework, landscaping/hardscaping, and building repair services.

This positioning supports:

- Clearer positioning
- Better search visibility
- Better conversion
- Easier maintenance
- Lower launch risk

## Suggested Demo-Site Direction

Build the demo as a clean, Vercel-ready concept site with:

- A concise homepage focused on property maintenance clarity.
- A services overview page that organizes the full service list without overwhelming users.
- Separate priority service pages for Snow & Ice Management, Parking Lot Maintenance, Excavation & Sitework, Landscaping & Hardscaping, and Building Maintenance & Repairs.
- A practical About page focused on company background only after facts are verified.
- A strong Contact page with phone, locations, hours, and a quote form.
- No inflated claims, fake reviews, unverified trust badges, or unsupported service-area expansion.

## Assumptions

- APS wants more qualified inbound leads from phone calls and quote requests.
- Commercial property maintenance should lead the positioning, while residential service availability remains included until validated.
- Snow and ice management is a priority business line because the current site has the deepest content and operational details for that service.
- The Lehigh Valley and listed nearby Pennsylvania areas are meaningful service areas, but the exact boundaries require confirmation.
- The New York address is still active and relevant, but this requires confirmation.

- Lead with a commercial property maintenance message and validate how much residential work should remain in the core pitch.
- Simplify the sitemap before writing final copy.
- Treat Snow & Ice Management as a flagship page.
- Move from keyword-stuffed SEO text to clean local SEO content with validated service areas.
- Use the pilot demo to show contrast: current clutter versus clear, credible service organization.
