# Pitch Summary

## Summary

American Property Solutions already appears to have the raw material for a stronger website: real services, local presence, year-round property maintenance positioning, and a broad set of exterior/property support capabilities. The current site could present that more clearly.

The opportunity is to show the owner a practical before-and-after: same business, cleaner message, better service organization, stronger quote path, and more credible local SEO.

## Confirmed Facts

- Business name: American Property Solutions.
- Current site: `https://aps-pa.com/`.
- Public phone: `610-760-0600`.
- APS publicly presents itself as a four-season property maintenance company.
- APS publicly lists services including snow removal, parking lot services, excavation/sitework, landscaping, hardscaping, building maintenance, repairs, concrete, drainage, sealcoating, crack sealing, and line striping.
- APS publicly lists a Walnutport, PA location and a Vestal, NY location.
- APS publicly mentions Lehigh Valley and surrounding Pennsylvania service areas.

## Observations

- The current website makes APS appear less focused than the service offering appears to be.
- The service list is broad, but it can be organized into a simpler property maintenance story.
- Repeated keyword blocks and spelling issues reduce credibility.
- The current site has contact options, but the path to a quote is not as clean as it should be.
- Property and building maintenance should carry the evergreen demo story, while snow and ice management should remain visible as an important seasonal service.

## Before: What the Current Site Communicates

- APS does many different services.
- The business has local service-area relevance.
- There are contact forms and a phone number.
- Some trust signals may exist, but they are not clearly validated for reuse.
- The site relies too much on repeated SEO phrases.
- The message can feel scattered, even though the services themselves are useful.

## After: What the Improved Concept Communicates

- APS is a year-round property maintenance partner.
- The company helps property owners, managers, businesses, and homeowners keep properties safe, functional, and maintained.
- Services are organized into five clear buckets:
  - Snow & Ice Management
  - Parking Lot Maintenance
  - Excavation & Sitework
  - Landscaping & Hardscaping
  - Building Maintenance & Repairs
- Visitors can quickly understand what APS does and how to request a quote.
- Local SEO becomes cleaner and more professional without repetitive keyword blocks.
- The site looks more credible because the structure, copy, and calls to action are simpler.

## Recommended Owner Conversation

Position the work as a low-risk improvement concept, not a criticism of the business.

Plain-language pitch:

The current APS website has the right ingredients, but the structure makes the service offering feel more scattered than it needs to. A cleaner site could position APS as a year-round property maintenance partner, organize the services into clear categories, remove repeated SEO text, and make it easier for commercial property owners, property managers, businesses, and homeowners to call or request a quote.

The goal is not to invent a new company. The goal is to present the existing company more clearly.

## Proposed Demo Angle

Show a simple Vercel demo with:

- A stronger homepage hero.
- Clear service category cards.
- A better quote path.
- Cleaner local service-area language.
- A proposed sitemap that is easier to maintain.
- Trust-signal placeholders marked for client approval.

## What This Supports

- Clearer positioning: APS becomes easier to understand quickly.
- Better search visibility: page-specific SEO replaces repeated keyword stuffing.
- Better conversion: phone and quote CTAs become more obvious.
- Easier maintenance: fewer scattered pages and clearer service groups.
- Lower launch risk: unverified claims stay out until approved.

## Assumptions

- Commercial property maintenance should lead the concept unless APS says otherwise.
- Property & Building Maintenance is the Phase Three flagship service; snow plowing should remain a supporting seasonal service.
- Residential services should remain included carefully until APS clarifies priority.
- Service areas should be confirmed before final public SEO work.
- Trust badges, testimonials, awards, certifications, guarantees, warranties, and years-of-experience claims require approval before final use.

## Recommendations

- Use the pitch to start a conversation through Nina or the friend connection.
- Show the current issue in practical terms: the business has useful services, but the website makes them harder to understand.
- Ask for validation on service priorities, service areas, trust signals, and audience focus before building.
- Keep the next step small: approve the demo direction, then build a clean concept site.

## Open Questions

- Would APS agree that property maintenance is the best umbrella position?
- Does APS want commercial work to lead the site?
- Is Property & Building Maintenance the correct evergreen flagship for the demo?
- Which service areas are current and profitable?
- Which trust signals can be shown publicly?
