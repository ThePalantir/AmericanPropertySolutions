> **Status update — August 7, 2026:** The earlier mixed-market direction in this file is superseded by the APS leadership-approved commercial-only direction in [commercial-only-revision-plan.md](commercial-only-revision-plan.md).

## Summary

American Property Solutions appears to be a year-round property maintenance company with a broad mix of exterior, site, parking lot, snow, landscaping, hardscaping, and repair services. The clearest pilot direction is to position APS as a practical property maintenance partner for commercial properties, property managers, local businesses, and residential property needs where appropriate.

The brief below separates confirmed public facts from assumptions that need client approval. It is intended to hand off to sitemap, copywriting, SEO, and design work without inventing claims.

## Observations

- APS has a stronger story than the current site structure suggests.
- Commercial property maintenance should be the lead theme unless the client says residential work is equally important.
- The public site contains valuable service information, but it needs to be cleaned, grouped, and rewritten.
- The existing service list is too wide for a simple navigation menu.
- Trust signals exist but need a validation pass before reuse.

## Assumptions

- The pilot should prioritize lead generation over recruiting, partner recruitment, or long-form company history.
- Snow & Ice Management is one of the highest-value services to emphasize.
- The demo site should support both commercial and residential audiences until the client confirms a narrower focus.
- The Pennsylvania address is the main location for the Lehigh Valley pilot concept.
- The New York location may support snow/service-area claims, but needs confirmation.

## Recommendations

- Use "year-round property maintenance" as the organizing idea.
- Make commercial property maintenance the first-read positioning.
- Keep service pages broad enough to avoid thin content but specific enough for search and conversion.
- Validate all trust signals before copywriting.
- Convert the service list into organized buckets instead of a long directory of contractor terms.
