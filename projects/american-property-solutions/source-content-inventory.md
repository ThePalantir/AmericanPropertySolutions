## Observations

- The source site contains broad service coverage, but it mixes service information with repetitive local SEO text and unvalidated trust claims.
- The useful content is primarily service scope, source-derived service areas, snow operations detail, partner/hiring prompts, and contact facts.
- The strongest demo structure is a service hub plus focused detail pages rather than a large homepage or keyword footer.

## Assumptions

- The local images in `website/public/approved-aps-assets/` are approved for demo use, but final usage rights and implied context still require launch approval.
- Service pages can use rewritten source-derived content as demo-safe copy if claims that need validation are documented.
- Partner and careers content should remain informational until form handling and public status are approved.

## Recommendations

- Keep all detailed services in centralized `website/lib/siteData.ts`.
- Use the service hub as the main navigation path instead of adding a large services dropdown.
- Add future partner/careers pages only after APS confirms public form routing, privacy needs, and active hiring or partner intake status.
- Do not render badge, rating, affiliation, or testimonial assets until APS approves exact wording and usage rules.
