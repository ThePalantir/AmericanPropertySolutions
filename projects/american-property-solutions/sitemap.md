> **Status update — August 7, 2026:** The earlier mixed-market direction in this file is superseded by the APS leadership-approved commercial-only direction in [commercial-only-revision-plan.md](commercial-only-revision-plan.md).

## Summary

This is a proposed pilot sitemap for American Property Solutions. It is designed to make the current broad service mix easier to understand, easier to maintain, and easier to convert into qualified leads.

The recommendation is to replace the current scattered service structure with a lean navigation centered on year-round property maintenance. The sitemap is not final and requires human/client approval before public launch.

## Observations

- The current site has too many service labels for a clean top navigation.
- Some current pages overlap, including building maintenance, remodeling, home repair, decks, patios, hardscaping, concrete, and parking lot work.
- Snow and ice management remains important as a seasonal service, but Property & Building Maintenance should be the evergreen flagship page for Phase Three.
- Service Partner Network and Careers may be useful but are not essential to the first sales-focused demo.
- Location pages are not recommended yet because confirmed service-area strategy is incomplete.

## Internal Linking Plan

- Home links to Services, Property & Building Maintenance, and Contact, with supporting links to seasonal snow and other service groups as needed.
- Services links to every core service page.
- Each service page links back to Services and Contact.
- Seasonal Snow Plowing links to Service Partner Network only if the partner program remains active and approved.
- Parking Lot Maintenance links to Excavation & Sitework where drainage, concrete, or site prep overlap.
- Landscaping & Hardscaping links to Excavation & Sitework where drainage, grading, patios, and hardscape prep overlap.
- About links to Contact and Services.
- Contact includes service-interest options that match the service page structure.

## Pages Not Recommended Yet

- Individual city location pages. Reason: service-area priority and exact geographic coverage are not yet confirmed; thin location pages would increase SEO and launch risk.
- Separate pages for every sub-service such as crack sealing, line striping, sod, patios, decks, concrete flatwork, driveway stone, and drainage. Reason: most would be thin unless APS provides detailed content, photos, and priority confirmation.
- Separate Remodeling Services page. Reason: it overlaps with building maintenance and repairs and may broaden the positioning too much.
- Separate Home Repair page. Reason: it may confuse commercial-first positioning unless residential repair work is a confirmed priority.
- Testimonials page. Reason: testimonials require approval and may work better as selected sections on relevant pages.
- Careers in primary navigation. Reason: useful but secondary to the first pilot goal of owner-facing lead generation.
- Service Partner Network in primary navigation. Reason: useful for operations/recruiting, but not central to the first demo conversion path.

## Assumptions

- The demo site should be sales-focused, not recruitment-focused.
- APS wants qualified quote requests and phone calls.
- Service pages should support real services already listed publicly, but final emphasis should wait for client validation.
- Commercial property maintenance should lead the structure while residential service fit remains available where accurate.

## Recommendations

- Use this sitemap as the first proposed structure for pilot v1.
- Get human/client approval before treating it as final.
- Keep the top navigation short and conversion-oriented.
- Build only the pages needed to show a stronger before-and-after concept.
- Avoid city/location pages until the service-area strategy is verified.
